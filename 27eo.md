# Weak Ed25519 public keys verify differently across ports. fiki-py (cryptography/OpenSSL) ACCEPTS a forged signature under the identity-point key: public key 0x01 followed by 31 zero bytes with signature 0x01 followed by 63 zero bytes verifies over any message (reproduced 2026-10-07 in py/'s uv env). The C# port (BouncyCastle) rejects small-order keys, reported as SignatureMismatch. Go's crypto/ed25519 and ed25519-dalek's non-strict verify are believed to accept like py, but that is unchecked. Impact is low in plain mode, because the forgeable identity is only the AID of the identity point, which no honest party holds; it matters where a resolver or registry could be made to hold such a key, and it is cross-port drift that no vector catches. Decide whether fiki refuses small-order keys (MalformedKey at keyid decode and resolver return), then add a vector for it at the next vectors_format bump and align every port.
